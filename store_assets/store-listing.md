# Google Play store listing

## App title

Video Downloader & Media Saver

## Short description

Find supported videos, choose quality, and keep your downloads organized offline.

## Positioning

Video Downloader & Media Saver is a free Android media downloader and download manager for media that users are authorized to save. It combines a built-in browser, verified media choices, recoverable tabs, quality selection, background downloads, and a clean local media library.

## Search language

Use phrases naturally where relevant: video downloader, media downloader, download manager, video saver, media saver, fast downloader, offline video, media manager, and video download manager.

Do not keyword-stuff, name protected platforms, or imply universal compatibility.

## Required notice

Only download media you own, have permission to download, or that is made available for downloading by the content provider.

## Quality-remake release description (publish only after device gates)

Find the video you intended to save. Verified results appear first, with uncertain sources in Additional results. Select a quality, preview a result, and report an advertisement or wrong video. Downloads are checked for readable tracks and expected audio before completion.

Keep your place with independent browser tabs, saved normal sessions and predictable Back navigation. Compatibility mode can relax website filtering while preserving detection. Library search, playback position, source-page reopening and batch actions help manage saved media.

Every capability is free, supported by clearly labeled ads. No subscriptions, credits, artificial speed limits or rewarded feature gates. Broader insights require consent, with access/deletion controls in Settings.

Supported coverage depends on the website and Android WebView. Progressive files and compatible unencrypted on-demand HLS/DASH are supported; live recording, protected content and universal website support are not offered. Do not claim guaranteed profitability or perfect detection. Preserve the existing brand and title.
