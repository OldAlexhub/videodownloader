# Google Play store listing

## App title

Video Downloader & Media Saver

## Short description

Download supported videos and media fast with quality selection and smart tools.

## Positioning

Video Downloader & Media Saver is a free Android media downloader and download manager for media that users are authorized to save. It combines a built-in browser, automatic media detection, real source-quality selection, background downloads, and a clean local media library.

## Search language

Use phrases naturally where relevant: video downloader, media downloader, download manager, video saver, media saver, fast downloader, offline video, media manager, and video download manager.

Do not keyword-stuff, name protected platforms, or imply universal compatibility.

## Required notice

Only download media you own, have permission to download, or that is made available for downloading by the content provider.
