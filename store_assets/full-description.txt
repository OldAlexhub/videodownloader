MODERN ANDROID STORAGE

Downloads use Android scoped storage. Save to the organized default media folders or choose a destination with the Android folder picker. Broad file access is not required.

PRIVACY AND CONTROL

Downloaded media stays on your device and is not uploaded to Old Alex Hub. Optional performance analytics can send limited text metadata, source domain, results, and ad impression events to help improve reliability. Analytics can be turned off in Settings. Google AdMob supports the free app and may process advertising data according to consent and device settings.

IMPORTANT

Only download media you own, have permission to download, or that is made available for downloading by the content provider. The app does not bypass DRM, authentication, subscriptions, paywalls, or access controls. Protected and unsupported media cannot be downloaded.

Compatibility depends on the website, media format, source availability, and Android device capabilities.
