Downloaded media stays on the Android device and is never uploaded to Old Alex Hub. Performance analytics and Search & website insights are on by default and can be disabled separately in Settings. Analytics sends limited text metadata, a random installation ID, source domain, performance results, and ad impression events to vd.server.oldalexhub.com. When both settings are on, submitted searches and visited domains from non-private sessions are used for aggregate reports. Full page URLs are not sent. Google AdMob may process advertising and device data. Users can disable either analytics setting and clear local data in Settings.
