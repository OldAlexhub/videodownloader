Downloaded media stays on the Android device and is never uploaded to Old Alex Hub. Optional performance analytics sends limited text metadata, a random installation ID, source domain, performance results, and ad impression events to vd.server.oldalexhub.com. The server derives approximate city and country when available, discards the request IP, and stores only a keyed hash of the installation ID. Google AdMob may process advertising and device data. Users can disable performance analytics and clear local data in Settings.
