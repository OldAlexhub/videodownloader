Downloaded media stays on the Android device and is never uploaded to Old Alex Hub. Optional performance analytics sends limited text metadata, a random installation ID, source domain, performance results, and ad impression events to vd.server.oldalexhub.com. Search & website insights is a separate opt-in, off by default; when enabled, submitted searches and visited domains from non-private sessions are used for aggregate reports. Full page URLs are not sent. Google AdMob may process advertising and device data. Users can disable either analytics setting and clear local data in Settings.
