# Feature graphic notes

Size: 1024 x 500 pixels

## Visual direction

- Use the supplied vivid blue and teal logo on a deep blue background.
- Keep the interface clean with one clear download motif and generous empty space.
- Use white typography with electric-blue and teal accents.
- Avoid screenshots that imply unsupported websites or protected services.

## Suggested copy

Video Downloader & Media Saver

Detect. Choose. Download.

Free with ads. No subscriptions.

## Compliance

Do not include third-party platform logos, protected-service names, copyrighted media stills, speed multipliers, DRM claims, or claims that every website is supported.
